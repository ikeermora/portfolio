Behind the Work

A more personal space for the ideas, routines, and interests that shape how I build, study, and think.

Why I build

Creativity is one of the values I admire the most in people. I try to keep myself creative, and to keep my mind busy trying new stuff. To create is one of the most beautiful things humans can do.

Beach

What I love most about the beach is the color of the water. The deeper and clearer the blue, the more I enjoy it. There is something about watching the waves reach the shore, disappear into foam. It is a simple scene, and I never get tired of it.

Soccer

Soccer fascinates me because it combines science and skill. I enjoy understanding the physics behind the movement of the ball, and I appreciate the technique and creativity required to control it. I think it's the most beautiful sport.

Ideas I keep returning to

I like minimalism. I believe that great work is always simple, beautiful, and precise.
